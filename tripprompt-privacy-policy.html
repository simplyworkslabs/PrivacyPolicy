TripPrompt Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026
Last updated: November 13, 2026

TripPrompt is an offline-first travel prompt library. SimplyWorks Labs does not operate a TripPrompt server and does not receive your prompts, placeholder values, usage history, or settings. The app stores this information locally on your device unless you deliberately copy or share prompt text.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the TripPrompt Android application (the “App”), developed by SimplyWorks Labs, handles information. It applies to the App itself. A store used to download the App, the Android operating system, and an application you choose as a sharing destination may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

2. Information the App handles

Information you enter or create

  • Custom prompt content: prompt titles, categories, descriptions, templates, and placeholders that you create or edit.
  • Prompt values: values you enter for placeholders, which may include a city, travel dates, hotel information, preferences, or any other text you choose to provide.
  • Reusable defaults: saved City, Start Date, and End Date values. The local settings store can also retain older non-core defaults created by a previous App version.

Local activity and preferences

  • Favorite prompt references and recently used prompt references.
  • Up to five recent text values per placeholder key, used as editing suggestions.
  • Home ownership/category filters.
  • Prompt font, font size, and reading-background preferences.
  • Locally generated custom-prompt identifiers and timestamps for creating, updating, favoriting, and recently using prompts.

Information the App does not access through declared permissions

The App does not declare permissions to access the camera, microphone, precise or approximate location, contacts, calendar, phone, SMS, nearby devices, photos, media, or general file storage. The App does not access advertising IDs or other device identifiers, and it has no account, login, authentication, advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting system.

You control what you type into prompts. Please avoid entering sensitive information that is not necessary for your use of the App.

3. How information is used

The App uses locally stored information to:

  • create, edit, display, search, and render travel prompts;
  • substitute values into prompt placeholders;
  • show favorites, recent prompts, and recent-value suggestions;
  • restore filters, defaults, and appearance preferences; and
  • copy or share prompt text when you request those actions.

SimplyWorks Labs does not use App information for advertising, profiling, tracking, or sale.

4. Local storage, transmission, and security

The App stores user-created prompts, favorites, recent prompt history, and recent values in a local Room database. It stores defaults, filters, and appearance preferences in local Android DataStore preference files. Bundled prompt templates are packaged with the App.

The App does not declare Android's Internet permission, contains no configured network endpoints, and does not upload App data to SimplyWorks Labs or another server. Cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of App files, databases, and preferences are explicitly disabled.

Local information is protected by Android's application sandbox and the security controls of your device. The App does not add application-level encryption to its Room database or DataStore files. No method of local storage is completely secure, so keeping your device software current and using an appropriate screen lock are recommended.

5. Clipboard and sharing

When you tap Copy, the displayed prompt text is placed on the Android system clipboard. Android and other applications with permitted clipboard access may process that text according to their behavior and privacy practices.

When you select Share prompt, the App opens the Android Sharesheet and supplies the prompt as plain text. The App does not choose a recipient or transmit the text itself. Information is disclosed to another service only after you choose a destination, and that destination's privacy policy then applies. Copying or initiating sharing also updates the App's local recent-prompt and recent-value history.

6. Android permissions

The production manifest declares no dangerous or runtime permissions. Android's manifest merger adds the following app-specific, signature-protected permission through the AndroidX libraries:

Permission Type Purpose
com.simplyworkslabs.tripprompt.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION Signature-level; no user approval Protects non-exported dynamic broadcast receivers used by AndroidX. It does not grant access to personal information, device sensors, files, location, or the Internet.

7. Third-party software and services

TripPrompt contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, authentication, cloud-storage, social-media, or network-service SDK. It uses Android/AndroidX application libraries from Google and Kotlin/Kotlinx libraries from JetBrains for user interface, navigation, local storage, serialization, and asynchronous processing. The App's implementation does not use these libraries to collect or transmit App data. For information about the library providers, see the Google Privacy Policy and JetBrains Privacy Policy.

If you share a prompt to a third-party application, that application is a service you select, not an embedded TripPrompt service. Review the selected application's privacy policy before sending information to it.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Custom prompts, favorites, defaults, and preferences remain locally until you change or delete them, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App.
  • The App retains up to 10 recent prompt references.
  • The App retains up to five recent text values for each placeholder key.

You can control or delete local information in the following ways:

  • delete a custom prompt from its Edit Prompt screen;
  • remove a favorite by toggling its favorite control;
  • clear core placeholder defaults and reset appearance settings from Settings;
  • clear persisted Home filters using Clear Filters; or
  • delete all App data, including recent history, through Android's system settings for TripPrompt, or by uninstalling the App.

Text already copied to the system clipboard or sent to another application is outside TripPrompt's storage. Use Android's clipboard controls or the receiving application's deletion tools to manage those copies.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA may provide rights to know, access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, and to receive a portable copy of certain personal information. California residents may also have rights concerning the sale or sharing of personal information. SimplyWorks Labs does not sell or share App data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Because the App does not send its local data to SimplyWorks Labs and has no account system, SimplyWorks Labs cannot remotely retrieve, identify, export, correct, or delete the information stored on your device. You can exercise control directly through the App and Android settings as described above. For questions about this policy or a privacy-rights request concerning information you believe SimplyWorks Labs possesses, contact simplyworkslabs@gmail.com.

10. Children's privacy

TripPrompt is a general travel utility and is not designed or directed specifically to children under 13. The App has no child-specific features, accounts, advertising, or developer-operated data collection. SimplyWorks Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App. A parent or guardian with a concern may contact SimplyWorks Labs using the address below.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when TripPrompt's practices or legal obligations change. The revised policy will show a new “Last updated” date. The revised policy will be published at the same public privacy-policy location used for the App.
