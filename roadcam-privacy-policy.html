RoadCam Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026
Last updated: October 5, 2026

RoadCam records video and optional audio to your device. SimplyWorks Labs does not operate a server that receives your recordings, location, or app settings. Google Play processes information needed for the optional Unlimited Recording purchase.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the RoadCam Android application ("RoadCam"), provided by SimplyWorks Labs ("we," "us," or "our"), accesses, uses, stores, and discloses information. It applies to the RoadCam app and does not replace the privacy terms of Google Play or your device manufacturer.

2. Information RoadCam accesses or creates

  • Camera video: RoadCam accesses the selected rear camera, front camera, or both cameras to provide previews and create dashcam recordings. Front and rear recording together depends on device support.
  • Microphone audio: When microphone permission is granted and audio recording is enabled, RoadCam includes audio in saved video recordings.
  • Recording information: RoadCam creates local filenames, recording timestamps, duration and file-size information, completion and active-state information, and the protected or unprotected status of recordings.
  • App preferences and entitlement information: RoadCam stores camera selection, segment duration, storage limit, whether the free-limit notice was acknowledged, cumulative free recording time used, and a locally cached Google Play purchase-entitlement state.
  • Device capability and operational information: RoadCam checks available cameras, concurrent-camera support, available storage, and device thermal status as needed to operate recording safely. This information is used locally by the app.
  • Purchase information: If you view, buy, or restore Unlimited Recording, RoadCam exchanges the product identifier, product details, purchase state, and purchase token with Google Play. RoadCam uses the purchase token locally to acknowledge the purchase through Google Play. SimplyWorks Labs does not receive your payment-card details through the app.

Recordings may incidentally contain people, voices, license plates, locations visible in the scene, or other personal information. RoadCam does not analyze recordings to identify these details. You are responsible for recording lawfully and for obtaining any consent required in your location.

RoadCam does not request device location permission and contains no app code that retrieves GPS or precise location. It does not use advertising identifiers, serve advertisements, provide analytics, or include a SimplyWorks Labs account or login system.

3. Storage and transmission

Video and audio recordings are saved in RoadCam's app-specific Movies directory on the device. Recording metadata is stored in a local Room database. Preferences, cumulative free recording usage, and cached entitlement state are stored locally with Android DataStore. RoadCam plays recordings from these local files and does not contain code that uploads recordings to SimplyWorks Labs or to a custom server.

RoadCam disables Android backup and device-to-device transfer for its app data and explicitly excludes files, databases, preferences, and external app storage from backup. Cleartext network traffic is disabled. Local recordings are not separately encrypted by RoadCam; their protection depends on Android's app storage controls and the security of your device.

Network access is used by Google Play Billing and bundled Google service components to retrieve product information, process or restore a purchase, acknowledge a purchase, and support billing operation and diagnostics. Those communications are handled by Google services under Google's policies.

5. Third-party services and libraries

Google Play Billing, Google Play services, and Google Data Transport. RoadCam uses Google Play Billing for the optional one-time Unlimited Recording purchase. Google processes purchase activity, product and entitlement requests, device and app information, network information, service interactions, and billing diagnostics as needed to provide and secure Google Play services. Google Play, not RoadCam, handles payment credentials. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

AndroidX and Kotlin libraries. RoadCam uses AndroidX CameraX, Room, DataStore, Compose, Lifecycle, Media3, and related Kotlin coroutine components for camera operation, local storage, interface rendering, lifecycle handling, and local playback. The RoadCam code uses these components locally and does not configure them as analytics or advertising services. Media3 plays app-owned local files; RoadCam does not provide it with network media URLs.

RoadCam does not include advertising, analytics, social login, cloud-storage, or crash-reporting SDKs.

6. Retention and deletion

Recordings remain on the device until you delete them, Android removes app data, or RoadCam's storage-management feature removes eligible older recordings. Automatic cleanup deletes the oldest eligible completed recordings and does not automatically delete protected recordings. You can delete an individual recording in the Recordings screen, including a protected recording after confirmation, or use Delete All to remove eligible unprotected recordings.

Local recording metadata is removed with its recording through the app's deletion flow. Settings, cumulative free recording usage, and cached entitlement state remain until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Deleting a recording does not reset cumulative free recording usage. Google retains purchase and service information according to Google's own retention rules; a Google Play purchase may be restored after reinstalling the app.

Because SimplyWorks Labs has no RoadCam account or recording server, we cannot remotely view or delete recordings stored on your device. To remove local app data, use RoadCam's deletion controls, clear RoadCam's storage in Android settings, or uninstall RoadCam. Requests concerning information held by Google should be directed through your Google Account controls or Google's privacy process.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or California, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or information about personal data processing, and to object or appeal where applicable. RoadCam does not provide SimplyWorks Labs with a user account or a remote copy of your recordings. Most RoadCam data can therefore be controlled directly on your device as described above.

Through RoadCam, SimplyWorks Labs does not sell personal information, share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use personal information for targeted advertising. To make a privacy request or ask a question, contact simplyworkslabs@gmail.com. We may need enough information to understand and respond to your request, but we cannot retrieve data that exists only on your device.

8. Security

RoadCam uses app-specific storage, disables app backup and device transfer, disallows cleartext network traffic, keeps its recording service and database service non-exported, and removes logging in release builds. No method of device storage or electronic transmission is completely secure. Keep your device updated, use a secure screen lock, and protect access to recordings that may contain sensitive scenes or conversations.

9. Children's privacy

RoadCam is a dashcam utility and is not directed to children under 13 or to children under the minimum age required by local law. RoadCam does not provide child-focused features, accounts, social features, or advertising. Adults using RoadCam are responsible for ensuring that any recording involving children complies with applicable law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when RoadCam's functionality, legal obligations, or third-party services change. The revised policy will show an updated "Last updated" date. Material changes will be communicated through the app, its store listing, or the location where this policy is hosted when appropriate.
