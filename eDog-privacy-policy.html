eDog Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026
Last updated: October 1, 2026

Privacy summary: eDog processes camera images and microphone audio on your Android device to detect motion and create recordings. SimplyWorks Labs does not operate a server that receives your recordings, camera feed, audio, or app settings. Google Play processes information needed for the optional Pro purchase.

1. Scope and developer

This Privacy Policy explains how the eDog Android application, package com.simplyworkslabs.edog, handles information. eDog is developed by SimplyWorks Labs.

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to simplyworkslabs@gmail.com.

2. Information eDog accesses or generates

Camera images and motion analysis

When you start monitoring, eDog uses the rear camera to display a live preview and analyze image brightness changes for motion. Camera frames used for motion analysis are processed on the device. They are not uploaded to SimplyWorks Labs.

Video and audio recordings

When motion is detected, eDog creates an MP4 video recording. If microphone permission is granted, the recording includes audio. A recording may contain faces, voices, private surroundings, or other personal or sensitive information captured by the device. Original recordings remain in eDog's app-specific storage unless you choose to export or share them.

Settings and recording metadata

eDog stores settings such as motion sensitivity, recording duration, automatic monitoring, app pinning, Night Mode, alert sound, and zoom level on the device. It also reads local file information needed to display recordings, including filenames, creation times, and file sizes. SimplyWorks Labs does not receive this information.

Purchase and entitlement information

The optional Pro unlock is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. eDog exchanges the product identifier, product details, pricing, purchase state, and purchase token with Google Play to start a purchase, confirm ownership, restore a purchase, and acknowledge a completed transaction. eDog does not receive your full payment-card details. The current app stores only a local Boolean indicating that a verified Pro entitlement exists; it does not persist the raw purchase token.

Information eDog does not access

eDog does not request location permission and does not use location APIs. The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics service, developer-operated account or login system, contact access, health-data access, advertising-ID access, or code that uploads recordings to a SimplyWorks Labs server.

Information you send by email

If you contact SimplyWorks Labs by email, SimplyWorks Labs receives your email address and the information you choose to include. That information is used to respond to your request and may be retained as reasonably necessary to manage the correspondence or comply with law.

3. Android permissions

The release version of eDog declares the following permissions, including permissions added by its production libraries:

Permission Purpose
android.permission.CAMERA Shows the rear-camera preview, analyzes frames for motion, and records video.
android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO Adds microphone audio to motion-event video recordings.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Displays the ongoing monitoring notification and its Stop action on supported Android versions.
android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE Runs a user-visible foreground service during active monitoring.
android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CAMERA Declares camera use by the foreground monitoring service.
android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE Declares microphone use by the foreground monitoring service.
android.permission.WAKE_LOCK Helps active monitoring continue while the device is not being interacted with.
android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Exports a recording to public media storage on Android 9 and lower only; the permission is capped at API 28.
com.android.vending.BILLING Connects to Google Play for the optional one-time Pro purchase and purchase restoration.
android.permission.INTERNET Added by Google Play Billing and its dependencies for Google service communication.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Added by Google Play Billing and its dependencies to determine network availability.
com.simplyworkslabs.edog.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION An app-specific signature permission added by AndroidX to protect non-exported dynamic receivers; it is not a user-granted permission.

4. Storage, retention, export, sharing, and deletion

  • Original recordings: retained in app-specific device storage until you delete one or all recordings, clear eDog's app data, or uninstall the app, subject to Android's storage behavior.
  • Settings and local entitlement: retained in private app storage until changed, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. eDog disables Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its data.
  • Exported recordings: exporting creates a separate copy in the device's public Movies/eDog collection. Deleting the original inside eDog does not delete that exported copy; delete it with the device's gallery or file-management tools.
  • Shared recordings: sharing occurs only after you choose a destination app. eDog grants that app temporary access to the selected file. The recipient app's handling, retention, and further sharing are governed by its own privacy practices.
  • Google Play records: purchase records are retained by Google according to Google's policies and your Google account settings.

5. Third-party services

Google Play and Google Play Billing

eDog uses Google Play Billing to offer and restore the one-time Pro unlock. Google may process Google account information, purchase and payment information, app and device information, network information, and service diagnostics as necessary to operate Google Play. Google's handling of that information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

eDog also uses AndroidX/Jetpack and Kotlin libraries for on-device application functions. The app does not configure those libraries as analytics or advertising services. Google states that the core Google Play services SDKs used transitively by Billing—base, basement, and tasks—do not collect end-user data; see Google's Google Play services data disclosure. The exact internal diagnostics processed by Google Play Billing are controlled by Google and are not visible in eDog's source code.

6. Network communication and security

No SimplyWorks Labs server endpoint is configured in eDog. Network communication in the release app is used for Google Play Billing and related Google services. Android cleartext network traffic is disabled. Google Play controls its own service endpoints and transport security.

Recordings and preferences are not encrypted by a separate eDog encryption layer. They rely on Android's app sandbox, platform access controls, and any storage encryption enabled by the device. The FileProvider used for sharing is not publicly exported and grants access only through explicit Android URI permissions. No security method can guarantee absolute protection, so protect access to your device and share recordings carefully.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

You control whether to grant camera, microphone, and notification permissions. You can stop monitoring at any time, revoke permissions in Android settings, delete original recordings individually or all at once, delete exported copies with system media tools, clear app data, or uninstall eDog.

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR/UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or information about the processing of personal data, and to object to or withdraw consent from certain processing. eDog has no developer-operated account or server database containing your recordings or settings, so most requests can be completed directly using the on-device controls described above.

The app contains no mechanism that sells personal information or shares it for cross-context behavioral advertising. To make a request concerning information sent directly to SimplyWorks Labs, such as a support email, contact simplyworkslabs@gmail.com. For information held by Google Play, use your Google account controls or contact Google under its privacy policy. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

8. Children's privacy

eDog does not provide accounts, social features, advertising, or child-directed features. SimplyWorks Labs does not knowingly receive children's personal information through an eDog-operated server because the app has no such server. However, a user can record a scene containing a child. The person operating eDog is responsible for using the camera and microphone lawfully, obtaining any required consent, and protecting recordings. If a child sends personal information directly to SimplyWorks Labs by email, a parent or guardian may request its deletion using the contact address below.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if eDog's functionality, legal obligations, or third-party services change. The updated policy will show a revised “Last updated” date. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate channel, such as the app's Google Play listing or an in-app notice when available and required.
