FormSnap Privacy Policy

FormSnap is a local-first Android document scanner. Document photos, edits, and PDF creation are processed on your device. SimplyWorks Labs does not operate a server that receives or stores your scanned documents.

Effective: October 1, 2026 Last updated: October 1, 2026 App: FormSnap Package: com.simplyworkslabs.formsnap

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how SimplyWorks Labs ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the FormSnap Android application ("FormSnap" or the "App"). It applies to the current version of FormSnap distributed through Google Play.

2. Information FormSnap Handles

Information Why it is used Where it is handled
Document photos and processed page images Capture, crop, rotate, enhance, preview, recover an active scan, and create a PDF. Locally on your device in App-private storage.
Scan and export metadata Maintain page order and settings, recover an active session, and display recent exports. Locally on your device.
PDF files Let you preview, save, share, and reopen recent exports. Created locally. A copy leaves App-private storage only when you choose a save or share destination.
Purchase and entitlement information Offer and restore the one-time permanent unlock for unlimited pages. Processed through Google Play Billing; entitlement status is also cached locally.

FormSnap does not require an App account. It does not request your name, email address, contacts, precise location, microphone recordings, or advertising identifier. The App does not include advertising or first-party analytics.

3. Permissions and Device Access

  • Camera: used only when you open the capture experience to photograph a document. FormSnap cannot capture a page unless you grant camera permission.
  • Internet and network state: may appear in the final App package through Google Play Billing and supporting Google components. They are used for Play services such as loading purchase details, completing purchases, and restoring ownership. FormSnap does not use them to upload your document images or PDFs to a SimplyWorks Labs server.
  • Files and documents: FormSnap uses Android's system document picker when you save a PDF. It does not request broad access to all files on your device.

4. How Information Is Used

FormSnap uses the information described above only to:

  • capture and display document pages;
  • detect document boundaries and apply your crop, rotation, and enhancement choices;
  • preserve and recover the current scan session on your device;
  • generate, save, share, and list recent PDF exports; and
  • process, acknowledge, cache, and restore the permanent in-app purchase entitlement.

We do not sell your personal information or document content. We do not use document content for advertising, profiling, or training artificial-intelligence models.

5. Local Processing and Storage

Capture, document-edge detection, perspective correction, image enhancement, and PDF generation run locally on your device. Active scan files, session metadata, preferences, recent-export metadata, cached PDFs, and the cached purchase-entitlement result are stored in App-private storage. FormSnap disables Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its private data.

When you choose Save PDF, Android lets you select the storage provider and destination. When you choose Share PDF, Android lets you select another app. A receiving app or storage provider handles the copy under its own privacy policy after you choose it.

6. Third-Party Services

FormSnap uses Google Play Billing for its one-time in-app purchase. Google may process purchase-related identifiers, product and transaction status, Google Play account information, device or App information, and technical diagnostics needed to provide and protect the billing service. Payment credentials are entered into and handled by Google Play; FormSnap does not receive your full payment-card details.

Google handles this information under the Google Privacy Policy. FormSnap also uses OpenCV and Android camera/PDF components locally for image and document processing; FormSnap does not use those components to send your document content to us.

7. When Information Is Shared

Document content is shared only when:

  • you explicitly save a PDF to a destination chosen in Android's document picker;
  • you explicitly share a PDF with an app or service you select; or
  • disclosure is required by applicable law and the information is available to us.

Because SimplyWorks Labs does not operate a document-upload service for FormSnap, we normally do not possess your document photos or PDFs and cannot disclose content that was never sent to us.

8. Retention and Deletion

  • Active scan files and metadata are kept locally as needed to edit or recover the current session. They are removed through the App's normal cancellation, deletion, completion, and cleanup behavior.
  • Recent-export metadata and usable cached PDF copies are kept locally so recent exports can be reopened. FormSnap limits recent history and routinely removes stale, incomplete, and unretained temporary exports.
  • You can delete pages while editing and remove entries from Recent Scans. Clearing FormSnap's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the App removes App-private scan data and cached exports from that device.
  • PDFs you saved or shared outside FormSnap are controlled by you and the destination provider or receiving app. Removing FormSnap does not delete those external copies.
  • Google retains purchase records according to its policies. After reinstall or local data loss, FormSnap may ask Google Play to restore ownership for the same Play account.

FormSnap has no user account or server-side document store, so there is no remote FormSnap account or cloud document collection for us to delete on your behalf.

9. Security

FormSnap uses Android App-private storage, a non-exported file-sharing provider with temporary access grants, disabled private-data backup, and blocked cleartext network traffic. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, and the security of files saved or shared outside FormSnap depends on the device, destination, and receiving service you choose.

10. Children's Privacy

FormSnap is a general-purpose document utility and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a SimplyWorks Labs server. If you believe a child has provided information directly to us, contact us so we can address the request.

11. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You can deny or revoke camera permission in Android Settings, choose whether and where to save or share a PDF, remove recent-scan entries, clear the App's local storage, or uninstall the App. Depending on where you live, you may also have legal rights concerning personal information we possess. Contact us to make a privacy inquiry. Because your documents remain on your device unless you choose an external destination, we generally cannot access or retrieve them for you.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when FormSnap's data practices or legal obligations change. We will revise the "Last updated" date on this page. Material changes will be presented as required by applicable law or Google Play policy.
