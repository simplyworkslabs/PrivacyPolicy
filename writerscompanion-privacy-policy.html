Writer's Companion Privacy Policy

Developer: SimplyWorks Labs
Effective date: November 3, 2026
Last updated: November 3, 2026

Writer's Companion is a local-first writing and reading application. Your writing, books, projects, tags, selected attachments, and preferences remain on your device unless you deliberately export or share them. The app has no developer-operated cloud service, account system, advertising, or analytics. Its only online integration is Google Play Billing for an optional one-time Premium purchase.

Scope

This policy explains how Writer's Companion version 1.0.0 handles information. It applies to the Android application distributed by SimplyWorks Labs. It does not replace Google's policies for Google Play, your device manufacturer's practices, or the policies of a storage provider or other application that you choose when exporting or opening a file.

Information kept on your device

The app processes information that you choose to create or import so it can provide its writing, reading, organization, search, and backup features. This may include:

  • Writing entries, titles, sections, tasks, drafts, snapshots, comparisons, captions, quotations, and notes.
  • Dates and times, favorites, archive state, optional user-entered location names, writing companions, event venues, organizers, speakers, and sessions.
  • Books and reading information, including titles, authors, ISBNs, editions, ratings, status, page counts, dates, reading pages, and minutes.
  • Projects, tags, memberships, progress values, and descriptions.
  • Photos, book covers, documents, edited image copies, file types, dimensions, checksums, and attachment captions that you select through Android's system pickers.
  • App preferences such as theme, writing font, font size, line spacing, paper background, timeline order, writing goals, and Writing Rituals.
  • Locally generated identifiers and search indexes needed to relate and find your records.

This content is stored in the app's private Room database, DataStore preference files, and private media directory. Temporary files may be created in the app's private cache while importing, editing, backing up, restoring, or exporting. The app does not send this authored content to SimplyWorks Labs or to Google Play Billing.

Optional location fields are text entered by the user. Writer's Companion does not request GPS or other device-location access.

Google Play Billing information

If you view, buy, or restore Premium, the app communicates with Google Play Billing. The app provides the Premium product identifier and receives product details, localized pricing, purchase state, acknowledgment state, and a purchase token. The token is used in memory to verify and acknowledge the purchase and is not written to the Writer's Companion database or ZIP backups. The app stores only a local boolean indicating whether Google Play most recently verified Premium ownership.

Google Play handles your Google account, payment method, transaction, purchase history, and related device, app, network, fraud-prevention, and diagnostic information under the Google Privacy Policy. SimplyWorks Labs does not receive your payment-card or bank-account details. Billing Library dependencies also include Google's diagnostics transport; the application does not provide it with your writing, books, or attachments.

Android permissions

The final release manifest declares only the following permissions. None is a camera, microphone, location, contacts, advertising-ID, or broad file-storage permission.

PermissionPurpose
com.android.vending.BILLING Connects to Google Play to show the Premium offer, complete a one-time purchase, acknowledge ownership, and restore purchases.
android.permission.INTERNET Contributed by Google Play Billing and its diagnostics dependencies for Billing communications. Writer's Companion has no developer-operated network endpoint.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Contributed by the Billing dependency to understand network availability for Billing and diagnostic delivery.
com.simplyworkslabs.writerscompanion.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION An app-signature permission added by AndroidX to protect dynamically registered, non-exported receivers. It does not provide access to user information or another device feature.

Photos and documents are selected through Android's user-mediated Photo Picker and document picker. The app receives access only to items you select and does not request general access to your photo library or shared storage.

Third-party services and software

Google Play Billing and Google Play services

Google Play processes Premium product, purchase, account, device, app, network, and diagnostic information to provide and secure purchases, confirm ownership, restore access, and operate its services. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play billing information.

On-device open-source libraries

The app uses AndroidX, Jetpack Compose, Room, DataStore, Hilt, Kotlin serialization, and Coil. These libraries implement the user interface, local storage, dependency injection, data encoding, and local image decoding. The Coil network module is not included, and no application endpoint or general-purpose HTTP client is configured. These libraries are not used by SimplyWorks Labs to operate an analytics or advertising service.

Exporting, sharing, and disclosure

Writer's Companion does not sell personal information, share information for cross-context behavioral advertising, or transmit authored content to SimplyWorks Labs. Information leaves app-private storage only when:

  • you explicitly export a ZIP backup or readable document to a destination selected through Android's document picker;
  • you open or share an attachment with another application using a temporary Android content URI; or
  • Google Play processes Billing and diagnostic information as described above.

Exported ZIP archives are not encrypted by Writer's Companion. The selected destination, storage provider, and receiving application control their copies. You are responsible for selecting a trusted destination and protecting or deleting exported files.

Retention and deletion

Local content remains on the device until you delete it using the available in-app controls, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Deletion safeguards may prevent removal of a book while writing activities still reference it, so related writing is not silently lost. Deleting a project removes its organization relationships rather than its member writing.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the app's private database, preferences, cached files, and managed attachments from that installation. Copies that you exported remain wherever you saved them and must be deleted there separately. Google retains and lets you manage Play purchase records according to Google's policies and account controls.

Android automatic cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for the app's files, database, and preferences. Writer's Companion instead provides user-initiated ZIP backup and restore.

Security

The app uses Android app-private storage, a non-exported FileProvider with temporary URI grants, validated and checksummed media imports, bounded file operations, and validate-before-write ZIP restoration. The manifest disables cleartext network traffic and Android automatic backup.

No security method can guarantee absolute protection. The app does not add application-level encryption to its Room database or managed attachment files; their primary protection is Android's application sandbox and the security of your device. User-created ZIP exports are intentionally unencrypted.

Accounts and login

Writer's Companion has no SimplyWorks Labs account, login, cloud synchronization, or user profile. Google Play may use the Google account already configured on your device for purchases and restoration, but the app does not receive your Google password or payment-card details.

Your privacy choices and rights

Because authored content is stored locally, you can review and correct it directly in the app, export it, or delete it using the app and Android storage controls. You can also decide not to purchase Premium; all core writing, reading, project, tag, search, and backup features remain available without a purchase.

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or information about personal data handled by a business. SimplyWorks Labs does not operate a server containing your app content and cannot access or remotely delete content stored only on your device. For privacy questions or information that you provide directly in support correspondence, contact simplyworkslabs@gmail.com. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Google account and Play purchase privacy requests must be made through Google's privacy and account controls.

Children's privacy

Writer's Companion is a general-purpose writing application. It has no child-specific mode, age-profile feature, social feature, advertising, or mechanism that asks a child to submit information to SimplyWorks Labs. The app does not knowingly collect children's authored content on a developer server because no such server exists. Parents or guardians with privacy questions may contact SimplyWorks Labs at the address below.
