ClockPro Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

SimplyWorks Labs ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how ClockPro ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

1. Information ClockPro Uses

Location Information

ClockPro can use your device's location to determine your approximate geographic location, display a city or locality, display the corresponding country information, and calculate local sunrise and sunset times. ClockPro is designed to work with approximate location; precise location is not required for these features.

Location access is requested only when needed for these features. You can deny location permission or disable it later through Android Settings. If location information cannot be obtained or resolved to a meaningful locality, ClockPro may display a generic location such as "Nearby Location."

Location and World Clock Data

ClockPro may store your selected or resolved clock locations and related settings locally on your device so that the App can restore your preferences. World Clock entries use the city coordinates and time-zone information needed to display their local time and calculate sunrise and sunset.

Purchase Information

ClockPro offers an optional one-time Premium purchase through Google Play Billing. Google processes the purchase transaction. ClockPro receives the information needed to determine whether the Premium entitlement is owned and to restore that entitlement. ClockPro does not receive or store your payment card number, bank account number, or other payment credentials.

2. Information ClockPro Does Not Collect

ClockPro does not operate its own advertising system and does not sell your personal information.

ClockPro does not use a weather service or Open-Meteo. Sunrise and sunset calculations are performed locally in the App and do not require a weather API.

ClockPro does not require an account, username, or password.

3. Network Access

ClockPro's core clock, location display, sunrise/sunset calculations, alarms, and World Clock features are designed to operate locally. Google Play Billing may use network communication to process and verify purchases.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

ClockPro stores applicable preferences and app data locally on your device. ClockPro does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Information handled by Google Play in connection with app distribution and purchases is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored ClockPro data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App, subject to Android's normal device behavior. You can also revoke location permission at any time through Android Settings.

6. Children's Privacy

ClockPro is not directed specifically to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by ClockPro. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when ClockPro's privacy practices or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will include a revised effective date.
