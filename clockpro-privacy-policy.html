ClockPro Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ClockPro (“the app”) handles information when you use it. Please review it before using the app.

1. Information handled by the app

ClockPro provides clock faces, alarms, timers, a stopwatch, night-mode and display settings, world-clock features, and weather-related information. Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, the app may process the following information:

  • Settings and preferences: Your selected clock face, backgrounds, themes, alarm and timer settings, and other app preferences.
  • Location: If you enable a location-dependent feature and grant location permission, the app may access device location to provide that feature, such as local weather. You can manage location permission in your device settings.
  • Network information: Features that retrieve weather or other online information require an internet connection. Your device communicates with the relevant service to obtain that information.
  • Purchase information: Premium purchases are processed through Google Play. The app may receive purchase and entitlement status needed to unlock Premium features; it does not receive your full payment-card details.

2. On-device processing and storage

App preferences and operational data may be stored on your device so that ClockPro can function and remember your choices. The app does not require you to create an account. Do not assume that app data is backed up or synchronized across devices unless your device or a separately identified service provides that functionality.

3. Photos, camera, and microphone

If a ClockPro feature requests camera, microphone, or media access, the permission is used to provide that feature. The app does not access these resources unless you use a feature that requires them and grant the relevant permission. Review Android’s permission controls to revoke access at any time.

4. Sharing and disclosure

ClockPro does not sell your personal information. Information may be transmitted to third-party services only as needed for features you choose to use, such as weather retrieval or Google Play purchase processing. Those services handle information under their own privacy policies. The app may also disclose information where required by law or where necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

5. Third-party services

ClockPro may rely on platform or third-party services, including Google Play and services used to retrieve online information. Their collection and use of information are governed by their own terms and privacy policies. Please consult the applicable provider policies for details.

6. Data retention and deletion

Information stored locally by the app generally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app, subject to Android and device backup behavior. Information handled by third-party providers is subject to their retention and deletion practices.

7. Children’s privacy

ClockPro is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the app or its operator, please contact us using the details below.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Your choices

You can manage app permissions through Android settings, disable optional features, and remove locally stored app data by clearing ClockPro’s app data or uninstalling it. Google Play manages purchase-related account and payment controls.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The “Last updated” date above indicates when the latest version was published. Continued use of the app after an update means the updated policy applies to your use going forward.
