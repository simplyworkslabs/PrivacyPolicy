StockAlert Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026
Last updated: November 10, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how StockAlert, an Android application provided by SimplyWorks Labs ("StockAlert," "we," "us," or "our"), handles information when you use the app.

In brief: StockAlert stores your monitored stocks, purchase prices, settings, and alert history locally on your device. It sends stock ticker symbols to Twelve Data to retrieve quotes and sends alert recipient addresses and alert contents to Resend to submit email alerts. StockAlert does not include advertising, analytics, user accounts, or social features.

Information StockAlert Handles

Information you provide or configure

  • Stock ticker symbols and company names.
  • Purchase prices, alert thresholds, and per-stock monitoring preferences.
  • One or more destination email addresses and a sender email address configured when the app is built. The current app displays the destination configuration in Settings but does not provide an in-app control to change it.

Information generated by the app

  • Current stock prices returned by Twelve Data, quote and retrieval times, and percentage changes calculated from purchase prices.
  • Alert history, including ticker, company, alert direction, alert level, purchase price, current price, percentage change, date and time, whether an alert was automatic or manual, and email status.
  • Monitoring status, enabled state, last attempt, last successful cycle, last price update, and concise error or result summaries.

Information StockAlert does not access

The app does not request or access your camera, microphone, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, media, files, calendar, call logs, SMS messages, advertising ID, or other device identifiers. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

How Information Is Used

StockAlert uses the information described above only to provide its stock-monitoring features, including displaying price changes, scheduling checks, evaluating alert thresholds, submitting requested email alerts, showing local notifications, preventing duplicate automatic alerts, retrying failed alerts safely, and displaying alert history and monitoring status.

Local Storage and Retention

Stock records and alert history are stored in a private Room database on your device. Monitoring status and timestamps are stored in private Android shared preferences. Recently retrieved quotes may also remain temporarily in memory while the app is running.

Android backup is disabled for StockAlert. Local records remain until you delete the associated stock, clear StockAlert's storage through Android settings, or uninstall the app. Deleting a stock from StockAlert also deletes that stock's associated local alert history. The app does not currently provide a separate control to delete an individual history entry or reset all data at once.

Third-Party Services and Data Transfers

Twelve Data

StockAlert sends requested stock ticker symbols and an API credential to https://api.twelvedata.com/ to retrieve quote data. Twelve Data will also receive network information ordinarily involved in an internet connection, such as an IP address. Twelve Data's handling and retention of information are governed by its Privacy Policy.

Resend

StockAlert sends email requests to https://api.resend.com/. These requests contain the configured sender and destination email addresses and the alert email content, which may include ticker and company name, purchase price, current price, percentage change, alert level or manual-alert status, and a delivery idempotency key. Resend's handling and retention of information are governed by its Privacy Policy.

StockAlert does not contain a separate SimplyWorks Labs application server. We do not use the app to sell personal information or for cross-context behavioral advertising. Third-party services may retain request or delivery records according to their own policies and the service account configuration used by the app.

Security

StockAlert stores its database and preferences in private app storage, disables Android backup, and disables cleartext network traffic. Production network requests use HTTPS. The app is designed not to write API credentials, email addresses, ticker symbols, purchase prices, or alert contents to its monitoring logs.

No security measure is perfect. API credentials configured into an Android application package may be recoverable by someone who obtains the package, and data transmitted to external services is subject to those services' security practices.

Your Choices and Deletion

  • You can disable automatic monitoring globally or for an individual stock.
  • You can deny notification permission; scheduled monitoring may still run, subject to Android requirements, but local alert notifications may not appear.
  • You can deny or revoke exact-alarm access and battery-optimization exemptions; this may reduce timing reliability.
  • You can delete a stock in the app, which also deletes its associated local alert history.
  • You can delete all locally stored StockAlert data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Deleting local app data does not automatically delete records already processed by Twelve Data or Resend. For those records, consult the linked third-party privacy policies or contact us for assistance.
