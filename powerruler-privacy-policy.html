PowerRuler Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026
Last updated: October 12, 2026

Overview

PowerRuler is an Android measuring utility provided by SimplyWorks Labs. It includes an on-screen ruler and an optional camera-based augmented reality (AR) measuring feature. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the app and the choices available to you.

SimplyWorks Labs does not operate an account system, advertising service, analytics platform, or application backend for PowerRuler. Measurements and camera images are not uploaded to SimplyWorks Labs.

Information processed by PowerRuler

Camera and AR information

If you choose AR Measure and grant camera permission, PowerRuler uses the live camera feed, device motion, tracking state, detected surfaces, anchors, and poses to estimate distances within the current AR session. The app does not take or save photographs or videos, and it does not upload camera images or measurements to SimplyWorks Labs. AR measurements and anchors are held only for the active measuring experience.

Settings and local app data

PowerRuler stores ruler preferences, display-calibration information, Pro entitlement state, and the number of free AR sessions used in app-private storage on your device. This information supports your selected ruler configuration, calibration, purchase access, and the per-installation AR trial. It is not sent to a SimplyWorks Labs server.

Purchase information

If you view, purchase, or restore PowerRuler Pro, the app communicates with Google Play Billing. Google Play handles the transaction and may process your Google account, purchase, payment, device, and diagnostic information under Google's policies. PowerRuler receives product information and purchase or entitlement status needed to provide the purchased feature. SimplyWorks Labs does not receive your payment-card details, and the app does not send purchase data to a SimplyWorks Labs server.

Information not accessed by app code

PowerRuler does not request access to your microphone, location, contacts, call logs, messages, photos, media library, or general file storage. The app does not access an advertising ID or create a SimplyWorks Labs account or profile.

Android permissions

Permission Purpose When used
android.permission.CAMERA Provides the live camera view and environmental tracking used by AR Measure. It also permits flashlight control while the camera is active on supported devices. Only when you open AR Measure and grant permission. Screen Ruler does not require camera access.
com.android.vending.BILLING Allows communication with the Google Play Store for product information, the optional one-time Pro purchase, purchase acknowledgement, and restoration. When the app checks entitlement or you interact with purchase or restore controls.

Third-party services

Google Play Services for AR (ARCore)

This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

ARCore provides motion tracking, surface detection, and anchor positioning. Google states that ARCore may collect app-install-specific pseudonymous identifiers, performance and diagnostic information, and ARCore API usage to operate, analyze, diagnose, and improve AR services. PowerRuler does not use ARCore Cloud Anchors or the ARCore Geospatial API. See Google's ARCore privacy requirements and Google Terms of Service.

Google Play Billing

Google Play Billing provides product details, processes the optional one-time PowerRuler Pro purchase, and reports purchase status to the app. Purchase and payment information is processed by Google rather than by a SimplyWorks Labs payment server. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

PowerRuler does not include a separate advertising SDK, developer-operated analytics SDK, social-network SDK, or cloud-storage SDK.

Storage, retention, and deletion

  • Ruler settings and calibration: retained in app-private storage until you change them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.
  • AR trial count: retained locally for the installation. It is excluded from PowerRuler's Android backup and restore rules. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes it from that installation.
  • AR session information: measurements, anchors, and tracking state are not maintained as a measurement history after the active experience ends.
  • Purchase entitlement: local entitlement state can be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling. The underlying Google Play purchase record is retained and controlled by Google and may be restored through Google Play Billing.
  • Support communications: if you email SimplyWorks Labs, your email address, message, and any information you choose to include may be retained as needed to respond, maintain support records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion using the contact address below.

You can delete PowerRuler's local data through Android's app settings by clearing storage, or by uninstalling the app. Data controlled by Google can be managed using your Google account and the controls described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Security

PowerRuler keeps its local records in Android app-private storage, which is protected by Android's application sandbox. The app does not operate a server that receives camera images, measurements, calibration information, or preferences. Communications performed by Google services are handled by those services under Google's security practices. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and SimplyWorks Labs cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or California, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or portability of personal information controlled by SimplyWorks Labs, and to object to certain processing. California residents may also have rights to know what personal information is collected, request correction or deletion, and opt out of sale or sharing. SimplyWorks Labs does not sell personal information or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and it will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Because PowerRuler has no account or developer-operated backend, SimplyWorks Labs ordinarily has no way to identify or retrieve your on-device app data. You can control that data directly by clearing storage or uninstalling. For information held by Google, use Google's privacy controls. For information you sent directly to SimplyWorks Labs, or for another applicable privacy request, contact simplyworkslabs@gmail.com. We may need enough information to understand and verify your request.

You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority where that right applies.

Children's privacy

PowerRuler is a general-purpose measuring utility and is not specifically designed or directed to children. The app does not provide accounts, social interaction, personalized advertising, or a mechanism for knowingly collecting children's personal information on a SimplyWorks Labs server. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to SimplyWorks Labs, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.
